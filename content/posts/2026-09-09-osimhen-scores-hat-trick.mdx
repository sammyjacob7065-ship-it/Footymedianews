Victor Osimhen scored a second-half hat-trick as Nigeria beat Benin Republic 4-0 at the Moshood Abiola National Stadium in Abuja, moving the Super Eagles top of their World Cup qualifying group with two games left to play.

The game was goalless at half-time, with Benin sitting deep and frustrating Nigeria's front line for long spells. That changed seven minutes after the restart when Osimhen turned in a low cross from close range to open the scoring.

## Second-half surge

He added a second just past the hour mark, meeting a corner at the near post, before completing his treble with a curled effort from the edge of the box in the 78th minute. Ademola Lookman rounded off the scoring in stoppage time.

Head coach spoke afterwards about the team's patience:

> "We knew it would take time to break them down. The players trusted the plan and it paid off in the second half."

## What it means for qualification

The win takes Nigeria to 16 points from seven games, two clear of their nearest rivals with two fixtures remaining. A win in their next match would all but confirm qualification with a game to spare.
